Who we are

🌐 We are a french spoken unit but open to english speaking players

Founded in 2015, Groupe Spécial de Reconnaissance et d'Intervention or special reconnaisance and intervention group is a group of players sharing a common vision of military simulation. We believe that immersion, cooperation, and efficient execution enable the success of complex and rewarding operations. We offer a demanding experience where every challenge is an opportunity to progress. GSRI cultivates a respectful and structured environment, where discipline and team spirit are the keys to our collective success.

If you're looking for operations where group synergy is paramount, you've come to the right place.
